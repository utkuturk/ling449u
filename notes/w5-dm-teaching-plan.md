## Exercise answers

- **Record:** [n √RECORD] versus [v √RECORD]; initial versus final stress,
  with accompanying vowel differences. Root identity is shared; categorial
  context affects realization and interpretation.
- **Separate:** [a √SEPARATE] versus [v √SEPARATE]; adjective /ət/ versus
  verb /eɪt/. Both retain initial primary stress. The adjective describes a
  state; the verb describes bringing it about.
- **Nominalizations:** arrival selects -al; collection selects -ion.
  Destruction shares the -ion pattern but uses the destruct- stem. Both
  -ion nouns also require stem–suffix phonology. Distinguish suffix choice
  from stem realization.

The speaker notes give fuller answers and references. The categorial
brackets abbreviate the structure; the pronunciation contrasts alone do
not decide between stored allomorphs and morphophonological rules.
